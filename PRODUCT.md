# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Trusted internal operators who need to control where automated web research is allowed to spend search and verification budget. They use the app as a focused admin tool: configure governed providers, prepare neutral search inputs, inspect candidate sources, and keep the eventual trusted source list defensible.

## Product Purpose

ReScrape identifies which public sites are worth extracting from before any extraction or export work begins. Success means the operator can enforce a provider whitelist, keep spend bounded, preserve source provenance, and require human approval before any source becomes trusted.

## Brand Personality

Deliberate, sober, exacting. The product should feel like a control room for budgeted research, with clear boundaries and no promotional gloss.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, decorative AI branding, oversized marketing heroes, playful crawler metaphors, and subject-specific language. The app must stay domain-agnostic and should never imply that AI verdicts are final truth.

## Design Principles

1. Controls before activity: make budget, scope, and provider boundaries feel primary.
2. Evidence over confidence theater: show provenance and state plainly when features later produce judgments.
3. Domain neutrality: labels and examples describe generic topics, sources, verdicts, and settings.
4. Human authority: terminal approval is always framed as an operator decision.
5. Quiet density: prioritize scanability, stable layout, and repeat use over visual drama.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard access for all controls. Motion should be minimal and state-driven, with reduced-motion users receiving no decorative transitions.
